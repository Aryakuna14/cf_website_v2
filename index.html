What is Maho?

U&I is a volunteer-driven NGO working with children from underserved communities. Volunteers provide after-school learning support, life skills training and career guidance.

Maho is a U&I centre for boys living in residential care in Bengaluru. Volunteers spend three hours with them every week on schoolwork, mentoring and activities.

Sessions at the shelter

Volunteers sit with the boys one-on-one and in small groups every week.

What we do

Three areas of work that help boys learn, cope and prepare for life after the shelter.

  1. 01

    Schoolwork

    Reading, writing and maths with a personal study plan for each boy.

  2. 02

    Confidence and wellbeing

    Small-group sessions where boys learn to talk about how they feel and look out for each other.

  3. 03

    Life skills

    Spoken English, managing money and career guidance for older boys preparing to leave the shelter.
