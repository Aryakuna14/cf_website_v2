About

What is Maho?

Maho is an all-boys U&I centre in Bengaluru for about 50 boys living in residential care. U&I volunteers teach them English every week.

U&I is a volunteer-run registered charity working with children from underserved communities across India.

In every class

What we bring

  • Role models & mentors
  • Personalised teaching
  • Learning materials
  • Creativity & play

Behind every class is a curriculum, textbooks and workbooks, life-skills workshops and centre celebrations. Across U&I that takes 5,348 volunteer teachers and leaders, and 19,500 hours of volunteer training.

Volunteer

Teach at Maho

Spend time every week with the boys at Maho, teaching English and being someone they can rely on. U&I gives its volunteers a curriculum, textbooks and workbooks, and training.

What volunteering at Maho involves

  • Teaching English to the boys, every week
  • Being a steady role model and mentor
  • Joining centre celebrations and life-skills workshops

Sign-ups go through U&I's volunteer page: choose your city and centre, fill in the application, then a review and interview.

#InThisTogether

Fund their dreams. Change their future.

Maho is part of #InThisTogether, U&I's biggest crowdfunding moment of the year: 6,000 students, 5,000 volunteers, 6 weeks. Last year, 29,000 supporters built the village for U&I's students.
