Where the money goes

For the children, across U&I and at Maho

Across U&I

Money raised from December to January supports U&I's work with children from underserved communities across India.

At centres like Maho

It also goes to U&I's centres, like Maho, home to about 50 kids in residential care, for the children's learning and wellbeing.

Behind every class

  • Curriculum for every class
  • Textbooks and workbooks
  • Life-skills workshops
  • Centre celebrations

Across U&I that takes 5,348 volunteer teachers and leaders, and 19,500 hours of volunteer training.
