About U&I

Who we are

U&I is a volunteer-driven organisation working to make quality education accessible to children from underserved communities.

Through dedicated volunteers, U&I creates learning spaces where children develop essential academic skills, build confidence and explore opportunities for a brighter future.

The focus is on meaningful connections between volunteers and children, built through consistent learning, mentorship and community engagement.

Maho is one of U&I's centres, in Bengaluru, for about 50 kids living in residential care.

What we teach

Beyond textbooks

U&I's classes build strong academic foundations and the skills children need in everyday life.

English
Reading, writing, vocabulary, grammar and communication, so children can express themselves with confidence.
Mathematics
Numerical understanding, logical reasoning and problem-solving, through structured learning.
Life skills
Communication, critical thinking, creativity, teamwork and self-confidence, through interactive, practical activities.
