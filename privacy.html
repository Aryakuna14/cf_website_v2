Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by volunteers of the U&I x Maho centre, part of U&I Trust, 8/1 Gover Road, Cox Town, Bangalore 560 005. For anything about your data, email info@uandi.org.in.

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, email address, phone number, the topic you pick and your message.

This website does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or advertising, and it loads nothing from other websites.

Like any website, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the service secure.

Why we use it

Only to reply to you and, if you asked, to arrange sponsorship, a donation, a partnership or volunteering. We do not sell your details or use them for marketing.

Who can see it

  • Our form provider, Formspree, receives your message and emails it to our team.
  • The U&I volunteers who answer messages.

Donations

Donations are made on U&I Trust's own donation page at uandi.org.in, not on this website. We never see your card or bank details. That page's own privacy policy applies to payments.

How long we keep it

We keep your message only as long as we need it to reply and follow up, then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the details you sent us. Email info@uandi.org.in and we will do it.

Children

This website does not collect information from children. Photos of the boys are used only with the shelter's consent, and their faces are covered. If you see anything that could identify a child, please tell us at info@uandi.org.in and we will remove it straight away.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
