Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by volunteers of the U&I x Maho centre, part of U&I Trust, 8/1 Gover Road, Cox Town, Bangalore 560 005. For anything about your data, email uandimaho@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. This website has no forms and no sign-ups. If you call or email us, we see your phone number or email address and whatever you choose to tell us.

This website does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or advertising, and it loads nothing from other websites.

Like any website, our hosting providers (Vercel and GitHub Pages) may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the service secure.

Why we use it

Only to reply to you and, if you asked, to arrange sponsorship, a donation, a partnership or volunteering. We do not sell your details or use them for marketing.

Who can see it

  • Only the U&I volunteers who answer our phone and email.

Donations

Donations are made by UPI, in your own payment app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or any other), to the Maho team's UPI account. The team passes every rupee on to U&I Trust. This website never sees or stores your payment details. The team sees only what UPI shows every payee: the name on your UPI account, the amount and the date.

How long we keep it

We keep your emails and messages only as long as we need them to reply and follow up, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the details you sent us. Email uandimaho@gmail.com and we will do it.

Children

This website does not collect information from children. Photos and videos of the kids are used only with the shelter's consent, and their faces are blurred or covered. We never share a child's name, age or class. If you see anything that could identify a child, please tell us at uandimaho@gmail.com and we will remove it straight away.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
